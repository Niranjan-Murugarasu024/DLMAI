RURAL HEALTH STATISTICS / HEALTH DYNAMICS OF INDIA
====================================================
Place these files here:

  district_wise_health_centres.pdf    — REQUIRED (what rhs_parser.py uses)
    Direct download URL:
    https://www.nhm.gov.in/images/pdf/monitoring/rhs/district-wise-health-centres.pdf
    This is the specific district-level table. rhs_parser.py was built and
    tested against this exact file format. No changes needed to the parser.

  health_dynamics_latest.pdf          — OPTIONAL (full 270-page bulletin)
    Find the latest edition at: https://mohfw.gov.in (search Health Dynamics)
    Formerly called "Rural Health Statistics". Useful for workforce vacancy
    data by district (doctors, nurses per PHC/CHC).
